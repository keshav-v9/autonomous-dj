# House-model playlists

## Popularity reference

`popular_house_candidates_2026-10-01.csv` is a dated metadata snapshot derived from the October 2026 Beatport Top 100 chart. It contains house-tempo candidates for ranking and evaluation only. It does **not** grant permission to download or train on the underlying commercial recordings.

## Licensed training audio

`../training/cc_by_house/manifest.csv` contains tracks from **1000 Handz — CC BY: FREE TO USE - Dance/House Instrumentals** on Free Music Archive. The audio is licensed under CC BY 4.0 and requires attribution to **1000 Handz / 1000Handz.com**.

The two datasets must not be merged as though they have the same rights: use commercial chart metadata as weak popularity labels, and use only licensed or separately authorized audio for waveform training.
